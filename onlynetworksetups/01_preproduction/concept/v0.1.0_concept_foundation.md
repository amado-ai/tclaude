## Title

**onlynetworksetups** by Amado A.I.

---

## Elevator Pitch

A first-person mobile game where players physically set up real home networking hardware —
modems, routers, cables, and mesh nodes — through hands-on interaction, learning how
networks actually work by doing the work.

---

## Core Pillars

1. **Real skills, real hardware** — Every interaction mirrors actual device setup procedures.
2. **Learn by doing** — No passive tutorials; knowledge is earned through trial, error, and feedback.
3. **Clear pass/fail** — Players always know why something worked or failed.
4. **Escalating complexity** — From one modem to a full mesh home network across nine missions.

---

## Target Audience

- Adults and young adults who want practical networking knowledge.
- Casual-to-mid gamers comfortable with first-person mobile games.
- People who have ever been confused by networking equipment.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By V1.0.0, a player who completes all nine missions will be able to:
- Identify coax, Ethernet, WAN, LAN, and power ports by sight.
- Connect a modem and router in the correct sequence.
- Name and place a Wi-Fi network.
- Diagnose intermittent failures and signal dead zones.
- Plan and place a mesh network for whole-home coverage.

---

## Mission Arc (9 Missions)

| # | Mission | Core Skill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First Signal | Coax to modem, modem online |
| 2 | Bring the Router Online | Ethernet modem→router, router Wi-Fi active |
| 3 | Wrong Port, Wrong Day | Port identification under pressure |
| 4 | Name the Network | SSID, password, band steering |
| 5 | Signal Wars | Router placement for coverage |
| 6 | Intermittent Failure | Diagnosing loose/wrong connections |
| 7 | The Dead Zone | Identifying coverage gaps |
| 8 | Mesh Rising | Adding and pairing mesh nodes |
| 9 | Whole-Home Certification | Full audit and certification terminal |
